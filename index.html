Privacy Policy – MapLeads Finder

Information We Process

MapLeads Finder processes search keywords, locations, public business names, website URLs, visited records, and scanning progress.

Data Storage

Extension settings and progress are stored locally in your Chrome browser using Chrome Storage.

Data Usage

Information is used only to find business websites, discover contact pages, prevent duplicate visits, and save scanning progress.

Data Sharing

We do not sell user data or share it with advertisers. MapLeads Finder does not use its own backend server.

Third-Party Websites

The extension interacts with Google Maps and external business websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Permissions

The extension uses broad website access (<all_urls>) to detect business website tabs, find Contact Us links, and redirect users to relevant contact pages. It also interacts with Google Maps to discover business websites.
